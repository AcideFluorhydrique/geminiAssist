geminiAssist is a simple WebView wrapper for Google Gemini. It blocks all URLs that are not essential for functioning.

You can switch on/off blocking by clicking the button at the top right.
If signing in gets stuck (for example at 2-step verification), switch blocking off, sign in, then switch it back on.
Swipe up a bit to hide the button.
Long press the button to reset the chat. This clears cookies, cache and site data, and signs you out.
